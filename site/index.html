Sponos

A nicer way to play music on Sonos, with Spotify built in.

Download for Mac

Latest version · macOS 12 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel · Free

  1. InstallOpen the download and drag Sponos to Applications.
  2. Allow the local networkClick Allow when macOS asks. That's how Sponos finds your speakers.
  3. Connect SpotifyOptional: see your playlists and liked songs. Search works without it.

Ask for music in your own words

New in version 0.14. Uses Claude, so it needs an Anthropic API key or a Vercel AI Gateway key, added once for the whole Sonos system.

  • NewDescribe it, get a playlistWrite something like "calm Friday afternoon, no vocals" and watch the playlist build song by song. Not quite right? Say what to change.
  • NewWhat should play next?Under the playing song, get a handful of songs that follow it well, each with a reason and a Play next button.
  • NewRooms in one placeTick rooms in and out of a group with a volume slider for each, or drag one room onto another.
